Frequently asked questions

Answers about using VirtuO. If you can't find what you are looking for, write to support@virtuo.run.

Basics

What is VirtuO for?

With VirtuO you can run an orienteering course without flags: after a race, when the controls have been collected, or a course you made yourself. The app watches your GPS position, and when you get within range of the next control it vibrates, beeps and records your split. It replaces both the flags and the SI card.

Does the app show an orienteering map?

No. You need a printed map to navigate: print the course map and take it with you. The app only signals the controls. The background map in the course view is for reference only and is not suitable for orienteering.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

You don't need a VirtuO account. Internet is only needed to download a course from Livelox. Punching controls works without internet while you run.

Which phones and watches does it work with?

It works on its own on iPhone and Android phones. If you have any smartwatch that shows your phone's notifications and vibrates for them, it signals there too. There is also a dedicated Apple Watch app, and with it you don't need to carry your phone.

How do I install the app?

The app is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Until then a beta test is running. If you would like to try it, write to feedback@virtuo.run.

Loading a course

Where can I get courses from?

In four ways: from Livelox (race courses), from an IOF XML 3.0 file (for example exported from Purple Pen or OCAD), from a GPX file with points, or by typing in the control coordinates by hand.

Why do I need to sign in to Livelox?

Livelox only lets signed-in users download courses into other apps. A free Livelox account is enough. You sign in on Livelox's own page; VirtuO never sees your password.

How do I sign in to Livelox?

In the app open the Livelox import and choose sign in. Livelox's own page opens and you sign in there; VirtuO never sees your password. Then you can search for an event and pick your class.

Why is there no course for a Livelox class?

You can only download courses that the organiser has made public on Livelox. For upcoming or ongoing races the course is usually published after the race. Try again later, or ask the organiser for an IOF XML file.

How do I export IOF XML 3.0 from OCAD or Purple Pen?

Purple Pen: use the XML export of the courses and choose IOF XML 3.0. OCAD: choose IOF XML 3.0 in the course export. Menu names may differ between versions. Then open the file with VirtuO (Share / Open with).

How do I import a GPX file?

Open the .gpx file with VirtuO (Share / Open with), or use the file import in the app. The file stays on your device.
Important: the GPX must contain separate points (waypoints or route points). The first point becomes the start, the last one the finish, and each point’s name becomes its control code. A recorded track from a run cannot be used as a course, because it has no separate points.

How do I make my own course?

Plan the course in Purple Pen or OCAD and export it as IOF XML 3.0. Or choose "Manual coordinate list" in the app and type in one control per line: code, latitude, longitude (for example 31 47.4995, 19.0423). Code S is the start, F the finish. Without codes the first line is the start and the last the finish. Print the map with the course and take it with you.

While running

How close do I need to get to a control?

15 metres by default. You can change the radius in Settings, even during a run.

What if the GPS misses a control?

Tap "Manual punch". If you want to skip a control or continue elsewhere, tap "Go to another control". These buttons are on the left page of the run screen.

How accurate is GPS in the forest?

In open terrain usually a few metres. In dense forest, deep valleys or next to cliffs it can be less accurate. That is why there is a radius, and why you can punch a control by hand.

Does it work in my pocket or with the screen locked?

Yes, if you set location permission to "Always" (iPhone) or "Allow all the time" (Android). Then the app detects controls even with the screen locked.

Battery tip

Continuous GPS uses battery. Charge your phone before a long run and enable low power mode if needed; you do not need to keep the screen on, as the app works with it locked.

Watches

How does the Apple Watch app work?

Courses you load on your iPhone are sent to the watch automatically. Start the run on the watch and you can run the whole course without your phone. The watch saves the run to Apple Health as a workout (heart rate, distance, route); this is on by default and can be switched off in the watch app settings.

Does it work with an Android smartwatch?

Yes. When you punch a control, VirtuO sends a notification to your phone, and your watch vibrates if it shows your phone's notifications. There is no separate Wear OS app yet.

Privacy and contact

Where does my data go?

Courses, runs and location data stay on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking. Details in the privacy policy.
